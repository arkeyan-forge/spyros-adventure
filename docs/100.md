# SSA: what "100 %" means, and what is left

> **Purpose:** the bar SSA must reach, what is done and what is left, by area · **Status:** current (2026-10-26) · **Tags:** [SSA]

Legend (every game): ✅ done, 🔧 in progress, ⏸ paused, ❌ not done. Owners: the user, the tester, the designer, the developers.

## Definition
- A player with their own disc installs the game through Skylauncher on macOS, Windows or Linux.
- Every mode of the original plays from start to end, with real and emulated portals and all figures.
- It is as smooth as the console or better.
- It never loses a save or a figure.
- Every part is documented.

## Done
- ✅ Boot, menus, new game, saves, the first levels, `level_027` at 30 fps (60 opt-in); movies (Bink 1) with the
  intro skip as an option.
- ✅ Emulated portal, Toy Box, shared collection, smart portal (the prompt opens the Toy Box), portal identity.
- ✅ macOS (Metal), played daily; the Linux kit (Skylauncher 0.3.1); kit 0.5.0 builds SSA and Giants.
- ✅ Skylauncher 0.3.1: install from the ISO, updates, one data folder, logs, report.
- ✅ Kit hygiene (`tools/kit_scan.sh`), identity rules, history rewrite for public files.
- ✅ One documentation tree in this repo; `SSALauncherTest` links
  (`runtime/CMakeLists.txt`).
- ✅ **Long-run stability** (2026-10-27): a 2-hour soak at `level_027` (isolated, `nice`, scripted presses walking and attacking, 30 fps
  limit): **no crash, no fault or `[guard]` line, 29.2-29.9 fps in every 10-minute window**, resident memory sampled every minute: **flat at
  about 816 MB** from the first minutes to 1 h 39 min (+/- 2 MB), then one step down to 345 MB that holds to the end (no level change and no frame
  drop at that moment: most likely the system compressing the pages under memory pressure, not the game freeing; no growth anywhere).
  The old heap crash (`sub_828A2360`, about 1 run in 10 in October) did not show: not in this run, nor in the 34 short isolated runs of
  the shader scan. The presses are those of `tools/pipelines/tour.sh`, repeated to 7300 s.
- ✅ **The 2 shaders Metal rejected** (2026-10-27): they are SSA's **grass vertex shaders** (they sample a wind texture in the
  vertex stage), drawn in 8 of the 32 Heroic Challenge levels (a scan of 34 short runs: `challenge_level_000-031`, `dnftest`, `level_000`; the story levels after `level_000` were not scanned) with no grass fringes on the islands. Vertex
  texture fetch is now translated and bound: **1122 of 1122 shaders translate** and the grass shows
  (`challenge_level_018` before / after). Metal only was run; the SPIR-V and DXIL builds compile, not run
  ([shaders.md](shaders.md), [docs/runtime/gpu/shaders.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/forge/blob/main/docs/runtime/gpu/shaders.md)).
- ✅ **Windows zlib** (2026-10-27, to confirm on a real PC): levels inflate through miniz (in the tree) with the same hooks as the system
  zlib on macOS and Linux; the Windows runtime cross-builds with `tools/build_windows.sh` and reaches `level_027` under Wine, headless, with no fault:
  the level's `mission.str` loads in 0.45 s instead of 0.75 s there ([zlib.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/forge/blob/main/docs/engines/lux/zlib.md)). Xbox UWP keeps the
  translated inflate (paused).

## Left
### Gameplay

### Figures and portal
- ❌ **Real portals, confirmed on hardware**: the Wii/PS3/PC portal on Windows (#11, the tester); the Wii U and Xbox One
  portals on the Mac (the user); the Xbox 360 portal (libusb).
- 🔧 **Every SSA figure type**: **done for what the game does with a figure on the portal** (2026-10-27): all 54 toy types
  (33 characters, 4 Legendary, 4 sidekicks, 8 magic items, 5 adventure packs) and 16 variants (one per kind) were made,
  placed in an isolated run and read by the game; the 37 characters were written back once (first use) with nothing outside the
  first pair changed (identity, variant, Giants' extra areas, trailers: byte for byte), checksums valid; items, packs and
  sidekicks are never written; the owner's real dumps cover exactly the 54 types
  ([figures.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/forge/blob/main/docs/runtime/host/figures.md#every-ssa-toy-type-by-type-2026-10-27-ssa-v),
  `tools/regress/ssa_figures.sh`, `tools/test_all.sh --long`). **Left:** XP or gold earned in a level, and an item's effect when
  used, were not reached in a scripted run (dev, or the tester by hand).

### Platforms
- 🔧 **Windows on a real PC**: the 0.3.1 portal and performance fix to confirm (#11, #12, the tester). The Windows kit
  has never run end to end on a real PC.
- ❌ **Linux on a real desktop**, and the AppImage; Steam Deck if possible (the user or the tester). Only containers so
  far.
- ✅ **Strict Vulkan drivers** (2026-10-28): the game ran on Vulkan (MoltenVK) with the Khronos validation layer and
  synchronization validation through the title, `level_027`, `level_040`, the Toy Box and the movies. Found and fixed:
  the PSIZE UBYTE4 attribute read as float (MoltenVK refused the pipeline, the null pipeline crashed), the null
  pipeline bound, one viewport per render target, copy-after-copy hazards. Left, explained: a MoltenVK descriptor
  limit report and a harmless colour output without attachment. Results and how to rerun:
  [plume_backends.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/forge/blob/main/docs/runtime/gpu/plume_backends.md#strict-vulkan-drivers-the-validation-layer-run-v-2026-10-28).
  Not proved here: a real strict desktop driver (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel on Windows or Linux), and one Vulkan-only
  rendering difference (blocks on the grass in `level_027`) is open there. (dev, the tester on a PC)
